## Global constraints

No autonomous mode activation during implementation. Explicit campaign config approval. Fail closed on uncertain provider outcomes and unsupported factual claims.

## Review focus
- Ambiguous send after network failure must never auto-resend.
- Replies arriving before follow-ups must cancel all pending actions atomically.
- Config or content changes must invalidate approval.
- Duplicate/concurrent tasks must not duplicate contacts, sends or calendar invitations.
- Expired Gmail history recovery must inspect tracked threads and preserve checkpoint correctness.
